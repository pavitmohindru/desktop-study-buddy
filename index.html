Questions, answered

Does it work on Mac or Linux?

Not yet. Desktop Study Buddy is built for Windows 10 and 11 because it reads the Windows taskbar and open windows.

How does my license work?

After you pay, you receive a license key by email. Enter it in the app under Unlock Premium. One key activates on up to 2 devices, and you can deactivate a device from the right-click menu to move it.

What if I am offline?

Premium keeps working for 7 days after the last successful check. Connect to the internet once in that window and the clock resets.

Is the Free version limited in time?

No. The Gravity Sovereign and the 15-minute study nudge are free for good.
